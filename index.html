About Me

I'm a third-year Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at Kyrgyz-Turkish Manas University in Bishkek. I'm passionate about robotics, embedded systems, and the creative side of engineering - turning ideas into real, working prototypes.

What drives me most is curiosity and the excitement of building something new. I love those moments of trial and error - when a circuit finally works after hours of testing, or when a small concept evolves into a functioning design.

I plan to continue my academic journey through a Master's and later a PhD, focusing on research and innovation. My goal is to contribute to developing technologies that make a real impact and eventually share that experience through teaching.

For now, I'm focused on learning, experimenting, and growing with every project I take on.

Autonomous Line-Following Robot

Started: February 2026 · In Progress

Currently designing and building an advanced line-following robot for an upcoming university robotics competition. The robot uses infrared sensors for precise line detection and incorporates obstacle avoidance capabilities using ultrasonic sensors.

Key Features:
• PID algorithm for smooth path tracking and speed optimization
• Real-time decision-making system for complex track scenarios
• Custom PCB design for better circuit integration and reliability
• 3D-printed chassis optimized for weight distribution and stability
